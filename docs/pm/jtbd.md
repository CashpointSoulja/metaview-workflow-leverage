# Jobs to be done

Hypotheses written as jobs. None of these come from interviews.

| Who | When | I want to | So I can | Evidence I would need |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CS onboarding lead | a deal closes | see every implementation fact with its source | start the kickoff without re-asking the customer | count of kickoff calls that re-ask known facts |
| AE | I close a deal | hand off without filling a long form | spend time on the next deal | minutes AEs spend on handoff today |
| CS manager | a packet has gaps | know who owns each gap and by when | stop gaps from blocking go-live | number of packets with unowned gaps |
| Business Operations | I pick what to build | compare options on a stated hypothesis | defend the choice and kill it if it fails | occurrence and time data per workflow |
| Security or legal reviewer | a customer records interviews | see the consent posture with its source | avoid launching without notice in place | share of packets with consent stated |
