# Discovery hypotheses and neutral interview guide

No interviews have been run. This is the plan I would use.

## Hypotheses to test

| ID | Hypothesis | Measure | Kill signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Implementation facts at close are spread across 3+ sources | sources per closed deal, sampled | most deals have one complete source |
| H2 | At least 1 in 4 packets has a contradiction in ATS or seats | contradictions ÷ packets reviewed | under 1 in 10 |
| H3 | CS spends 60+ minutes per enterprise packet assembling facts | minutes per packet, timed, by segment | under 20 minutes |
| H4 | A named owner and dated checkpoint improves follow-through | checkpoints met ÷ checkpoints set | no difference vs today |
| H5 | Configuring the CRM covers most of the value | facts captured by config ÷ facts needed | config covers 80%+ |

## Neutral interview guide

Ask about the last real instance, not opinions about a tool. Don't describe the lab before the end.

1. Walk me through the last deal you handed over or received. What happened first?
2. Where did you find the hiring system, seat count and go-live date for that deal?
3. Was anything missing or different between sources? What did you do?
4. How long did getting to a kickoff-ready state take? How do you know?
5. Who decided when something was unclear?
6. What happens today when onboarding goes worse than planned? Who notices, and when?
7. What have you tried to fix this? What happened?
8. If this stayed exactly as it is for a year, what would it cost you?

Avoid: "Would it help if...", "Don't you think...", pricing, or naming any vendor.
