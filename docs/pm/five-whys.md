# Five whys (hypothesis chain)

This is my reasoning chain to test, not a diagnosis of Metaview. I have no internal data.

1. **Why might onboarding start slowly after a deal closes?** Hypothesis: CS has to rediscover implementation facts the AE already heard.
2. **Why would they rediscover them?** Hypothesis: the facts live in free-text notes and email, not in required CRM fields.
3. **Why not in required fields?** Hypothesis: adding mandatory fields slows the AE at close and gets filled with placeholders.
4. **Why does it matter if a fact is wrong rather than missing?** Hypothesis: a wrong ATS or seat count produces a wrong integration plan, which costs more than asking.
5. **Why not just automate the handoff?** Because automation that guesses on contradictions turns a visible gap into a hidden error. The root design choice I am testing is: extract with citations, abstain on conflict, and make a named person own the gaps.

What would prove this wrong: if discovery shows CS already gets complete, consistent facts at close, or the handoff is a small share of onboarding time, I should pick a different workflow.
