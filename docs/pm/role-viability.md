# Role viability

What the public Business Operations posting asks for, and what this concept shows. Quotes and their sources are in `docs/research.md`.

| The role asks for | What I show here | What this doesn't show |
|---|---|---|
| Leverage over headcount | A ranked choice by leverage per build day, with the formula visible | Real volumes or time data |
| Identifying cross-functional workflows | Eight candidates across five teams, two excluded for product overlap | Internal priorities |
| AI-native, pragmatic solutions | A deterministic evidence checker with a clear slot for a model behind the same gate | A production model integration |
| Build vs buy judgment | Weighted options with unknowns kept as upper bounds | Vendor quotes |
| Reusable patterns | Exported playbook and audit | Adoption by real teams |

I have not worked at Metaview and have no inside knowledge of its workflows.
